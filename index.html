How to use Soosh

  1. Ask Stubby for ideas. Stubby is Soosh’s activity helper. Tell it what you’d like to do, or ask for ideas.
  2. Check the details. Read what the activity needs. Check its time, price and location before deciding.
  3. Make a plan. Choose a time and add the activity to your calendar. Complete your account setup to invite someone.

Make a plan you can return to

Add an activity to your calendar. After you try it, log a Moment if you want. You choose who sees each Moment.

Questions

Can I use Soosh on my own?
Yes. You can make a plan for yourself or invite someone after completing account setup.
Do I need an account?
An account is needed to make plans and return to them.
How do at-home suggestions work?
Start with an interest you’ve chosen. Stubby can suggest a step from its activity guide.
What do the activities cost?
Some have a fee or need supplies. A missing price means the cost is unknown.
Does Soosh book tickets?
Bookings and payments happen with the provider. Check their price and availability before booking.
