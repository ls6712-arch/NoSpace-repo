Questions

What is Soosh?
Soosh is a free app for finding hobbies and things to do, and making plans with friends. Pick something to try, put it on your calendar and earn Stubs for what you finish.
How do I find a new hobby?
Start with something you already like or have wondered about, and try one small first step this week. Each Corner shows things to try, how to start and what you’ll need.
What can I do with friends this week?
Cook a meal together, take a photo walk, go to a class or a run club, or start a shared Pursuit like learning a song. Make a plan and tap Bring someone.
What is a Corner?
A Corner is an interest, like pottery, running or guitar. It has things to try, how to start, what you’ll need and walls of what people are making.
What is a Stub?
A Stub is a keepsake on your Shelf for something you did. You earn one when you finish a Pursuit, go to an event a host confirms, or log Moments in one Corner on 10 different days in a month.
Is Soosh free?
Yes. Soosh is free to use and runs in your browser. Some activities cost money, like supplies, classes or tickets. A missing price means the cost is unknown.
Do I need a goal to get started?
No. You can explore an interest first. Start a Pursuit when there’s something you want to keep working toward.
Who can see what I post?
You choose for each Moment: Only you, Followers or Everyone. You can block or report anyone.
Do I need an account?
You’ll need an account to make plans and return to them.
Does Soosh book tickets?
Bookings and payments happen with the provider. Check their price and availability before booking.
