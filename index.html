Before you start

What can I do on Soosh?
Find hobbies and activities to try, make plans and work toward personal goals called Pursuits. Explore on your own or do something with someone.
What is a Corner?
A Corner brings activities together around an interest, such as drawing, cooking or photography. Available guides can help you find a first step and see what you’ll need.
Do I need a goal to get started?
No. You can explore an interest first. Start a Pursuit when there’s something you want to keep working toward.
Do I need an account?
You’ll need an account to make plans and return to them.
What do the activities cost?
Some have a fee or need supplies. A missing price means the cost is unknown.
Does Soosh book tickets?
Bookings and payments happen with the provider. Check their price and availability before booking.
