Weekly schedule for hobbies and friends: a simple template

Use a realistic weekly schedule template for hobbies, friendships and free time without turning leisure into another chore.

How to make a weekly schedule

Start with fixed commitments. Then choose one hobby session, one optional social activity and time to rest. Keep travel and preparation time in mind.

A simple weekly template

Monday: choose an activity and check supplies. Wednesday: 20-minute hobby session. Friday: message a friend with two possible plans. Weekend: try one activity and leave time unscheduled.

How to make plans stick

Choose a small first session, add a backup time, and make the next step specific. If plans change, move the activity rather than treating the week as a failure.

Try it this week

  1. Choose one activity or goal you genuinely want to try.
  2. Check the time, cost and supplies you need.
  3. Put a realistic first session on your calendar.
  4. Reflect on what you enjoyed and plan a next step.
