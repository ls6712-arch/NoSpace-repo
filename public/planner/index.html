Where to start

The best activity plan is small enough to follow. Choose one activity, decide when and where to do it, and keep a backup option for busy days.

Try this simple plan

  1. Choose one activity for the coming week
  2. Put a specific time on your calendar
  3. Prepare supplies or check any booking details
  4. Reflect briefly afterward and decide on the next step
