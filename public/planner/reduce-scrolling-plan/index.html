Replace scrolling with activities: a practical weekly plan

Create a realistic plan for spending some screen time on hobbies, movement, creativity or social activities.

Where to start

Instead of aiming to eliminate screen time, choose one recurring moment when you would rather do something active or creative.

Try this simple plan

  1. Identify one routine scrolling window
  2. Choose a hobby that takes less than 20 minutes to start
  3. Prepare materials before that time arrives
  4. Review what felt enjoyable without judging yourself

Make it happen with Soosh

Explore activity ideas and plan a manageable next step. Soosh helps you move from inspiration to doing.
