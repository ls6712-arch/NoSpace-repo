How to plan activities with friends

Organize shared hobbies and weekend activities with a simple plan for interests, budgets and schedules.

Where to start

Shared activities are easier to organize when everyone knows the plan, cost and timing. Offer two clear options rather than an open-ended group chat.

Try this simple plan

  1. Ask friends for availability and approximate budget
  2. Suggest two specific activities
  3. Confirm meeting details and responsibilities
  4. Keep a backup plan for cancellations
