What is Soosh?

Soosh is a hobby discovery and activity planning platform that helps people find things to do, make plans, and work toward personal goals. It brings inspiration and planning into one place so ideas can turn into real experiences.

What can you use Soosh for?

Discover hobbies
Explore creative, active, social and beginner-friendly activities based on your interests and resources.
Plan activities
Choose an activity and make time for it in your schedule.
Build a social life
Find ideas for shared activities and make more concrete plans with friends.
Work on personal goals
Use Pursuits to make progress on something you want to keep doing or learning.

Common questions

Is Soosh a hobby app?
Yes. Hobby discovery is one of its main uses, alongside activity planning and personal goals.
Can I use Soosh to find things to do when bored?
Yes. Start with things to do when bored and choose a practical next step.
Can I use Soosh to plan activities with friends?
Soosh provides planning tools and ideas for shared activities. Check the current app for the available collaboration features.
Do I need an account?
An account is needed to save plans and return to them.
Does Soosh book activities or guarantee prices?
No. Bookings and payments happen with providers. Confirm prices and availability before committing.
