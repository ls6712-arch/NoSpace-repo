Things to do in Bangalore with friends: weekends and after work

Bengaluru has a lot more to do than malls, brunch and pubs. Most of the best options are free or cost less than a meal out, and many run every week, so you can go once and keep going back.

Free things to do on a weekend morning

  • Read in Cubbon Park with Cubbon Reads. A silent reading meetup that started in January 2023 and runs on Saturdays from 9am to 2pm. There is no sign-up: bring a book and a mat or sheet, and check the group’s Instagram for the exact spot that week. You can bring friends, as long as you keep the talking low.
  • Join a run club. Bengaluru’s run clubs are mostly free and meet on Saturday or Sunday mornings. Stride Run Club runs around Agara Lake in HSR Layout on Sundays, BEL Bullets meets in Cubbon Park, and Indiranagar has its own club. Many end with breakfast nearby, which is where people actually get talking.
  • Walk Lalbagh or Cubbon Park at the same hour each week. Early mornings have regulars: walkers, yoga groups, sketchers and birders. Going at the same time is how you start to recognize faces.

After work, on a weekday

  • Bookshop talks and launches. Champaca, in Indiranagar and on Edward Road in Vasanth Nagar, hosts author conversations, book launches and readings, usually in the evening or on weekend afternoons.
  • Rangoli Metro Art Center. Right beside the MG Road metro station, with three galleries, an auditorium and an open area for performances and workshops. Easy to fit in after work if you commute by metro.
  • Board game nights. Cafés in Koramangala and Indiranagar run regular board game meetups, and the hosts will teach you the rules. It is one of the easiest ways to meet people, because the game gives you something to talk about.

Worth a small spend

  • A pottery workshop. Studios in Indiranagar, Domlur, JP Nagar and Jakkuru run one-day wheel and hand-building sessions for beginners. Book a slot with a friend, and ask whether the studio has open hours for when you want to go back.
  • A day trek. Skandagiri and other hills within a couple of hours of the city are popular for sunrise treks. Since April 2026, every official trekking route in Karnataka has to be booked in advance on the Forest Department’s Aranya Vihaara portal, and groups must go with a certified nature guide, so plan ahead instead of just turning up.
  • A weekly class. Dance, climbing, a language or music. A class on the same evening each week puts you next to the same people, which a one-off event can’t do.

How to make it a plan, not just an idea

  1. Pick one thing from this list and put it on a specific Saturday or weekday evening, not “sometime”.
  2. Send the plan to one friend with the date already chosen. People say yes to a date far more often than to “we should do this”.
  3. Go four times before you decide whether it’s for you. The first visit is almost always the awkward one.
  4. Plan around traffic. Something 15 minutes away that you will actually go to beats something better across the city.

How Soosh helps

On Soosh, pick a Corner like pottery, running or reading and you get things to try, a first step and what you’ll need. Make a plan for a free evening, bring a friend or go solo, and log a Moment when you’ve done it. Hosts run Spaces for their studios and clubs and post events you can go to, and when a host confirms you came, you get a Stub.
