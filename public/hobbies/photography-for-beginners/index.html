Photography for beginners

Start a photography hobby with a phone or camera and learn to practice observation, light and composition.

Where to start

You can start photography with a phone you already own. Practice taking pictures in different light before buying new equipment.

Try this simple plan

  1. Photograph the same subject in morning and afternoon light
  2. Try three different angles
  3. Notice the background and framing
  4. Pick one photo and explain what you like about it
