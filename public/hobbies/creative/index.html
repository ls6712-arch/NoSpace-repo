Creative hobbies for adults

Explore drawing, pottery, photography, journaling and other creative activities with approachable beginner ideas.

Where to start

Creative hobbies give you space to experiment. You can start with a small project rather than investing in expensive equipment or formal classes.

Try this simple plan

  1. Pick one medium that interests you
  2. Try a small project with basic supplies
  3. Save what you made as a reminder of progress
  4. Repeat with one new technique next week
