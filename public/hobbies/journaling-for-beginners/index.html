How to start journaling

Simple journaling prompts and a realistic routine for beginners who want a creative reflective hobby.

Where to start

Start journaling with just a notebook or digital document. Write for five minutes about your day, an idea or something you want to try.

Try this simple plan

  1. Choose paper or a private digital note
  2. Set a five-minute timer
  3. Use the prompt: What caught my attention today?
  4. Revisit it twice this week without aiming for perfection
