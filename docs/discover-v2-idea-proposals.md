Status: **proposed, waiting for founder approval.** Source: `src/app/data/cornerIdeas.ts`.
Nothing on the Discover page reads these yet (that is PR 2).

## The rule used

Each value is worked out from the Corner's own guide traits in `cornerGuides.ts`, so the
founder can change the rule or single rows.

- **Time:** a 15 minute first step (`quick`) fits an hour and tonight. Experiences, projects
  and helping fit the weekend only. Outdoor Corners fit tonight and the weekend. Everything
  else fits tonight.
- **Spend:** an experience fits Under $25 and Treat me. Anything you can start with what you
  have at home (`haveIt`) fits Free and Under $25. The rest fit Under $25.
- **Who:** a solo Corner fits Solo. A together Corner fits A friend and New people. Either
  fits Solo and A friend.
- **Idea title and how:** placeholders. The title is the Corner's name and the how line is
  the guide's first step. Final copy is the founder's to approve.
