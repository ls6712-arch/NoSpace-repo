## Exclusions and quality safeguards

Do not target medical treatment terms such as depression treatment or anxiety medication simply because of search volume. Avoid thin, mass-produced pages, fabricated local availability, or claiming Soosh features that are not shipped. Prefer one useful canonical page per search intent, with clear internal links to discovery, planning, social activities and Pursuits.
