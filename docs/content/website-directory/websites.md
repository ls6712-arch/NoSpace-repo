# Specialist websites for Soosh users

Draft outbound-link shortlist, prepared Nov 5, 2026. These are known reputable candidates, not sites whose current availability, terms or every item have been legally certified in this session.

## What Soosh would publish

Use each site’s name, the original short description below and a normal link to its public page. Linking to a legitimate source is different from copying its content. A website does not need to be copyright-free for users to visit it. Do not infer permission to use thumbnails, articles, videos, audio, downloads or embedded viewers.

No copyrighted artwork, site logos, screenshots or excerpts have been copied into this pack. No claim that every resource is free, requires no account, is available everywhere or is suitable for every age has been made.

## Optional prompts and rights notes

### The Public Domain Review
Browse a collection, choose one detail and use it as a starting point for a sketch.

Rights note: Historic collection items and the site’s own essays can have different rights. Do not treat the entire site as copyright-free.

### Closer to Van Eyck
Choose a small area of a painting and notice how color, edges and texture are used.

Rights note: Public viewing does not grant permission to reproduce conservation imagery.

### Smithsonian 3D
Rotate an object and draw it from more than one viewpoint.

Rights note: Some Smithsonian assets have open-access designations; verify the individual asset before reusing it.

### Obelisk Art History
Choose one artist and compare how they handled the same subject in different works.

Rights note: Link to the project. Do not assume its writing or images are free to republish.

### Europeana
Explore one theme across several collections and save links to the sources you like.

Rights note: Rights differ between items and supplying institutions; an aggregator listing is not a reuse license.

### Biodiversity Heritage Library
Choose a botanical plate and study its shapes before drawing a plant of your own.

Rights note: Historic pages can have different copyright statuses by item and jurisdiction. Link-only in this shortlist.

### Standard Ebooks
Choose an opening chapter, read a little and decide whether you want to continue.

Rights note: Public-domain status can differ by country. Do not import books or covers into Soosh based on this listing.

### Poetry Foundation
Read a poem, then write a few original lines about an ordinary object.

Rights note: Many poems are copyrighted and displayed with permission. Link to them rather than copying them.

### The Marginalian
Follow one essay to a book or artist you want to explore further.

Rights note: The essays and illustrations are not automatically licensed for republication.

### Chrome Music Lab
Try a short rhythm and then attempt to play a version on your instrument.

Rights note: Link to the interactive tools. Do not copy their code, branding or recordings without checking applicable terms.

### musictheory.net
Choose one concept you are learning and try it on your instrument afterward.

Rights note: The educational material remains subject to the site’s terms. Listing a link is not a reuse permission.

### Exploratorium Science Snacks
Choose a relevant light or color activity and read its instructions and requirements before starting.

Rights note: Use an outbound link. Do not copy the activity instructions, diagrams or photos into Soosh without permission or an applicable license.

### Miniature Engineering Craftsmanship Museum
Choose an object and study how its maker joined small parts or represented details.

Rights note: Museum and maker images may be copyrighted. Link to the museum rather than republishing them.

### Plants of the World Online
Check a plant’s accepted name before looking for a care guide suited to your conditions.

Rights note: A botanical reference is not a universal plant-care guide. Text, images and data may have different terms.

### Macaulay Library
Explore a species you recognize and listen for differences between recordings.

Rights note: Recordings and images can belong to contributors. Do not download and rehost them based on this listing.

### Old Maps Online
Compare an old map with a place you know before deciding on a suitable present-day route.

Rights note: The map provider controls image rights. Old maps do not verify current access, routes or venue availability.

## First release suggestion

Start with 6 to 8 links spanning art, reading, music and nature. Use the existing Corner tags and Soosh’s own icons rather than copied website images. Keep the visit optional. Reuse these links as research or inspiration resources, not as fabricated events or local recommendations.

Before publishing the directory, open each current destination, check relevant site terms and practical access, and review its suitability for the intended audience. Link checks should not sign up, buy anything or bypass restrictions. If future plans involve reproducing content, assess the individual item and license separately.
